Privacy Policy for RemindMee Notes

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Developer: Swapnil Gadewar. Contact: ssgswapnil07@gmail.com

The short version

RemindMee Notes keeps your notes on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, and no tracking or analytics. We do not receive your notes, your contacts, your location, or your recordings.

What the app stores, and where

Your notes, checklists, reminders, labels, tagged contacts, tagged places and attachments are stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database. Notes you place in the private vault are additionally locked with a key that is protected by your vault password, your recovery answer and, if you turn it on, your fingerprint. We cannot open them, and we cannot recover your password.

The optional backup file you create yourself is encrypted with a passphrase you choose and is saved where you choose. We never receive it.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

The app asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can turn any of them off at any time in your phone's settings.

  • Microphone: to turn your speech into text when you dictate a note. The text comes from your phone's own speech recognition service (usually Google's), which may send audio to its servers under its own privacy policy. RemindMee does not record or keep audio.
  • Camera: to take a photo and attach it to a note.
  • Contacts: to let you tag a note to a person and to find the person you mention. Contact names are kept on your phone.
  • Location, including "Allow all the time": to remind you when you arrive at a place you tagged, even when the app is closed, and to show your position on the place picker. Your location is used on your phone and is not sent to us.
  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Exact alarms: to fire reminders at the minute you chose.
  • Display over other apps and a foreground service: to show the floating note bubble while you use another app. It stops when you close it.
  • Bluetooth and Wi-Fi information: to start a reminder when you connect to or leave a Bluetooth device or Wi-Fi network you chose. Reading a Wi-Fi network's name requires location access on Android.

Information that leaves your phone

RemindMee has no servers of its own and does not send your notes anywhere. Two features contact other services:

  • Maps: the place picker loads map pictures from OpenStreetMap, which sees your internet address and the map area you view. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.
  • Place search: when you type a place name to search, the text you type is sent to your phone's location service (provided by Google Play services) to find coordinates.

We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Because there is no account and no copy on our side, you delete your data by deleting notes in the app or by uninstalling the app, which removes everything stored on the phone. Backup files you created are yours to delete.

Security

The database is encrypted on the phone, and private vault notes have a second layer of encryption. No method is perfectly secure, and anyone with access to your unlocked phone could see notes that are not in the private vault. Keep your phone locked.

Changes
